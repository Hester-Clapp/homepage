The class 379 is a now retired train from the Electrostar family, built by Bombardier in 2011 and operated by Greater Anglia on West Anglia routes to Hertford, Stansted and Cambridge. In January 2022 they were all replaced by class 720 trains, primarily due to their high lease cost, and are currently in storage awaiting conversion to battery-powered trains.

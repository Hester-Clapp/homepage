The class 720 is a new train of the Aventra family, which operates regional and commuter services out of Liverpool Street.

Quick Facts:

Manufacturer: Bombardier
Operators: Greater Anglia, c2c (future)
Entered service: 2020
Retired: No
Replaced: class 317, class 321, class 360 and class 379
Coaches: 5
Capacity (Greater Anglia):
- 490 seats (Standard Class)
- 145 standing
- 4 bikes
- 2 wheelchairs

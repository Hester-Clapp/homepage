London Transport Challenge

Rules

Start at King's Cross (or your local London terminus) at 10am. The challenge will usually take around 8 hours.

Generate your stations, and try to visit them all. You must leave or arrive at each of your stations by train or rail-replacement bus, and take a selfie of you at platform level and outside.

You may use any public transport, including:

  • London Underground
  • London Overground
  • Elizabeth Line
  • Docklands Light Railway
  • London Trams
  • Any National Rail trains
  • London Bus
  • Riverboat services
  • Cable Car

You may not use private transport, such as:

  • Santander Cycles
  • E-Scooters
  • Taxi or ride share services
  • TfL Dial-a-ride

You may make use of maps, timetables, journey planners and other publically available information.

Advice

Inform yourself about strikes and engineering works beforehand; you may want to complete the challenge on days with minimal disruption.

I would recommend taking a pencil and paper to plan your route before you rush off onto a train.

Generally, allow 1 hour per station, plus 1 for lunch/snacks and another for things going wrong.

If you use travelcards, note that sometimes the gates will not let you back into a station that you have recently exited. If this happens, speak to a member of staff who will let you through. The ticket will usually continue to work afterwards.

Personally, I like to eat in local businesses rather than fast food chains. Half of the fun is ending up in random parts of London and finding out what is there.

Most importantly, enjoy yourself! Enjoy the route planning, the experience of travelling, the problem solving in tight situations and the sense of achievement at the end. Don't stress too much about completing everything as quickly as possible.
